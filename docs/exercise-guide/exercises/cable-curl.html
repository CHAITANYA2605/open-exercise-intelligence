Cable Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

The cable curl uses a low-pulley cable to maintain constant tension on the biceps throughout the full range of motion, including at the bottom where free weights lose tension. This makes it particularly effective as a finishing biceps exercise and is commonly performed with a straight bar or rope attachment.

Primary muscles

biceps brachii long head
biceps brachii short head
brachialis

Secondary muscles

None listed

Equipment

Cable Machine (Low Pulley)

Starting position

  • Stand facing a low pulley cable machine holding a straight bar or EZ-bar attachment. Arms extended downward, elbows at the sides.

Setup

  • Set the pulley to the lowest position. Step back slightly so there is tension on the cable at full arm extension.

Steps

  1. Curl the bar upward by flexing the elbows, keeping upper arms stationary.
  2. Raise until full contraction, then lower under control back to the starting position.

Coaching cues

  • Feel the constant tension throughout the full range
  • Keep upper arms pinned to sides

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on the descent.

Range of motion

  • Full extension at the bottom, full contraction at the top.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Allowing the elbows to drift forward
  • Using body swing

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
