Preacher Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

The preacher curl is a biceps curl variation performed with the upper arms resting on an angled arm pad, which prevents elbow movement and places the biceps under constant tension. The preacher bench position provides a greater stretch on the long head of the biceps at the bottom and prevents cheating with the torso.

Primary muscles

biceps brachii long head
biceps brachii short head
brachialis

Secondary muscles

None listed

Equipment

EZ-Curl Bar
Preacher Bench

Starting position

  • Sit at the preacher bench with the upper arms resting flat on the angled pad. Hold an EZ-curl bar or dumbbells with a supinated or semi-supinated grip. Arms nearly fully extended.

Setup

  • Adjust the bench seat height so the upper arms lie flat on the pad with the armpits at the top edge of the pad.

Steps

  1. Curl the bar upward by flexing the elbows, keeping the upper arms in contact with the pad.
  2. Raise until full biceps contraction is achieved.
  3. Lower slowly and under control — the eccentric portion is especially important here.

Coaching cues

  • Keep the upper arms welded to the pad
  • Control the descent — do not let the bar drop

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on descent.

Range of motion

  • Near-full extension at the bottom (avoid hyperextension), full contraction at the top.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Lifting the upper arms off the pad to gain leverage
  • Not lowering to full extension, missing the stretch

Safety

  • The preacher position can stress the distal biceps tendon at full extension — control the descent

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
