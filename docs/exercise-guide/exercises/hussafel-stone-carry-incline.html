Husafell Stone Carry on Incline

strengthexpertcompoundcarrybilateral

A husafell stone carry performed on an inclined surface such as a ramp or steep hill. The incline dramatically increases the difficulty as the athlete must both carry the stone and fight against gravity on the slope. This variation is used in highland games and specialty strongman events.

Primary muscles

erector spinae
transverse abdominis

Secondary muscles

gluteus maximus
rectus abdominis

Stabilizers

internal oblique

Equipment

Slam Ball

Starting position

  • Set up for Husafell Stone Carry on Incline in proper strongman position.

Setup

  • Prepare all equipment and body position for Husafell Stone Carry on Incline.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Deadlift husafell stone to bear hug position.
  2. Step 2: Begin walking up incline with stone pressed against body.
  3. Step 3: Take short controlled steps managing the increased effort demand.
  4. Step 4: Reach top of incline and set stone down safely.

Coaching cues

  • Shorter steps on incline
  • Stone stays against body throughout

Breathing

  • Brace core maximally before every lift. Breathe between reps if needed.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion specific to the implement being used.

Tempo

  • Explosive concentric phase, controlled eccentric.

Common mistakes

  • Taking too large steps on incline
  • Leaning stone away from body

Safety

  • Use collars on all loaded barbells.
  • Ensure clear path for carries.
  • Have spotters for overhead movements.

Training

  • strength: 1–5 reps
  • power: 1–3 reps
  • strength: 3–6 sets
  • power: 3–5 sets
