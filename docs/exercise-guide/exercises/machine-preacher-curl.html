Machine Preacher Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

The machine preacher curl replicates the preacher bench position using a fixed cam-driven resistance curve. The upper arms rest on a fixed pad and the hands grip a handle attached to the machine, which maintains constant tension throughout the curl. The machine removes the need to stabilize the weight and allows the lifter to focus entirely on the bicep contraction, making it effective for hypertrophy-focused training.

Primary muscles

biceps brachii short head
brachialis

Secondary muscles

biceps brachii long head

Equipment

Suspension Trainer (TRX-style)

Starting position

  • Sit at the preacher curl machine with the upper arms resting on the pad. Grasp the handles with a supinated or neutral grip.

Setup

  • Adjust the seat height so the upper arms rest fully on the pad with shoulders relaxed.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with arms extended to the starting angle of the machine.
  2. Step 2: Curl the handles upward by flexing the elbows.
  3. Step 3: Hold at the peak for one second.
  4. Step 4: Lower slowly back to the starting position.

Coaching cues

  • Arms on the pad throughout
  • Feel the stretch at the bottom
  • Squeeze at the top

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full extension to full flexion as allowed by the machine.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Lifting the arms off the pad
  • Rushing the eccentric

Safety

  • Machine design varies — check the full ROM on the first rep

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
