Incline Dumbbell Hex Press

strengthintermediatecompoundpushbilateral

The Incline Hex Press performs the dumbbell squeeze press on an incline bench, directing the adduction and pressing force toward the clavicular (upper) head of the pectoralis major. It combines the unique constant-squeeze stimulus of the hex press with the upper chest emphasis of an incline angle.

Primary muscles

pectoralis major clavicular
pectoralis major sternal

Secondary muscles

deltoid anterior
triceps lateral head

Stabilizers

pectoralis minor

Equipment

Dumbbell (Fixed)
Adjustable Bench (Incline / Decline)

Starting position

  • Set an adjustable bench to 30-45 degrees. Lie back and hold two hex dumbbells pressed firmly together with a neutral grip, positioned above the upper chest.

Setup

  • Set incline to 30-45 degrees. Use hex-sided dumbbells to prevent rolling. Press dumbbells together before each set.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Hold dumbbells squeezed together above the upper chest.
  2. Step 2: Lower under control to the upper chest while maintaining inward squeeze.
  3. Step 3: Press up and slightly back to the starting position.
  4. Step 4: Squeeze hard throughout; do not let the dumbbells separate.

Coaching cues

  • Squeeze the dumbbells together as if your life depends on it
  • The harder you squeeze, the more upper chest activation

Breathing

  • Inhale before lowering. Exhale pressing up.

Range of motion

  • Light touch of upper chest at bottom; full extension at top.

Tempo

  • 3-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Bench angle too steep (above 45°), shifting load to shoulders
  • Losing the squeeze through fatigue

Safety

  • Use lighter dumbbells than your standard incline press — the squeeze adds significant demand
  • Hex or flat-sided dumbbells only

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
