Wide-Grip Barbell Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

The wide-grip barbell curl uses a grip wider than shoulder width on a straight barbell, which internally rotates the shoulders slightly and shifts greater emphasis to the short head of the biceps. The short head contributes to the width and peak of the bicep when developed well. It contrasts with close-grip curls which emphasize the long head.

Primary muscles

biceps brachii short head

Secondary muscles

biceps brachii long head
brachialis

Equipment

Standard Barbell

Starting position

  • Stand upright holding a barbell with a supinated grip wider than shoulder width. Arms extended at the thighs.

Setup

  • Grip wider than shoulder width — approximately 1.5x shoulder width. Keep the elbows slightly forward.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Curl the barbell upward by flexing the elbows, keeping the upper arms at the sides.
  2. Step 2: Curl until maximum elbow flexion is achieved.
  3. Step 3: Lower the bar slowly back to the starting position.

Coaching cues

  • Wide grip, short head focus
  • Elbows at the sides
  • Control the descent

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full extension to full flexion.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Swinging the torso
  • Elbows flaring excessively outward
  • Using momentum

Safety

  • Avoid if wide grip causes wrist discomfort — use an EZ-bar instead

Training

  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
