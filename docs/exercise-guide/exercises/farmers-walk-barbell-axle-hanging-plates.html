Farmers Walk with Axle Bar and Hanging Plates

strengthexpertcompoundcarrybilateral

A farmers walk using an axle bar (thick bar) with plates hung from chains rather than loaded directly on the bar. The hanging plates oscillate during the walk, creating a dynamic stability challenge not present with standard farmers walk implements. This builds extraordinary grip and core strength.

Primary muscles

trapezius upper
erector spinae

Secondary muscles

transverse abdominis
gluteus maximus

Equipment

Standard Barbell

Starting position

  • Set up for Farmers Walk with Axle Bar and Hanging Plates in proper strongman position.

Setup

  • Prepare all equipment and body position for Farmers Walk with Axle Bar and Hanging Plates.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Set up axle bar with plates hanging from chains at appropriate height.
  2. Step 2: Grip bar with both hands using double overhand grip.
  3. Step 3: Deadlift bar and walk with control, managing plate oscillation.
  4. Step 4: Set down carefully as oscillating plates make lowering challenging.

Coaching cues

  • Smooth controlled steps minimize swing
  • Crush grip on axle throughout

Breathing

  • Brace core maximally before every lift. Breathe between reps if needed.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion specific to the implement being used.

Tempo

  • Explosive concentric phase, controlled eccentric.

Common mistakes

  • Walking too fast increasing oscillation
  • Soft grip on thick axle

Safety

  • Use collars on all loaded barbells.
  • Ensure clear path for carries.
  • Have spotters for overhead movements.

Training

  • strength: 1–5 reps
  • power: 1–3 reps

  • power: 3–5 sets
