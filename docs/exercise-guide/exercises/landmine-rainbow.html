Landmine Rainbow

strengthintermediatecompoundrotationbilateral

A landmine exercise where the barbell is swept in a wide arc from one side of the body to the other, tracing a rainbow shape overhead. The anti-rotation demand is significant as the core resists the shifting weight through the arc. This builds rotational stability and power.

Primary muscles

internal oblique
external oblique

Secondary muscles

rectus abdominis
transverse abdominis

Stabilizers

erector spinae

Equipment

Standard Barbell

Starting position

  • Set up for Landmine Rainbow in proper position.

Setup

  • Prepare body and equipment for Landmine Rainbow.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Stand with landmine bar held at chest height with both hands.
  2. Step 2: Sweep bar in a large arc from one hip up and over to the other hip.
  3. Step 3: Keep arms relatively straight and move from the core.
  4. Step 4: Reverse the arc to return to starting side.

Coaching cues

  • Drive the arc from hips and core rotation
  • Smooth continuous arc

Breathing

  • Breathe steadily; never hold breath during static holds.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion with control at all times.

Tempo

  • Controlled throughout; hold positions deliberately.

Common mistakes

  • Using mostly arms instead of core
  • Excessive spine bend

Safety

  • Build prerequisite strength before attempting advanced variations.
  • Stop if joint pain occurs.

Training

  • strength: 1–5 reps
  • hypertrophy: 5–10 reps
  • strength: 3–5 sets
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
