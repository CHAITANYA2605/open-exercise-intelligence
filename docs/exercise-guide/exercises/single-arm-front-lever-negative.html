Single-Arm Front Lever Negative

strengthexpertcalisthenicspullunilateral

A front lever regression where only one arm grips the bar while the body is lowered from vertical to horizontal. The negative (eccentric only) allows training the front lever position with one arm at a time. This is an intermediate step toward the one-arm front lever.

Primary muscles

latissimus dorsi
rectus abdominis

Secondary muscles

transverse abdominis
erector spinae

Stabilizers

biceps brachii long head

Equipment

Pull-Up Bar (Doorframe)

Starting position

  • Set up for Single-Arm Front Lever Negative in proper position.

Setup

  • Prepare body and equipment for Single-Arm Front Lever Negative.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Hang from bar with one hand. Place other hand on wrist for support initially.
  2. Step 2: Engage lat of working arm and slowly lower body to horizontal.
  3. Step 3: Maintain rigid body throughout the negative.
  4. Step 4: Lower fully then reset by using both hands to return to start.

Coaching cues

  • Full front lever mastery required first
  • Control the rotation carefully

Breathing

  • Breathe steadily; never hold breath during static holds.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion with control at all times.

Tempo

  • Controlled throughout; hold positions deliberately.

Common mistakes

  • Rotating body during the negative
  • Trying without full two-arm front lever

Safety

  • Build prerequisite strength before attempting advanced variations.
  • Stop if joint pain occurs.

Training

  • strength: 1–5 reps
  • hypertrophy: 5–10 reps
  • strength: 3–5 sets
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
