Hanging Windshield Wiper Bent Knee with Pause

strengthintermediatecalisthenicsrotationbilateral

A windshield wiper variation with bent knees and a deliberate pause at each end range of rotation. The knee bend reduces the lever arm making this accessible before the straight-leg version, while the pause maximizes oblique time under tension at the most challenging point.

Primary muscles

internal oblique
external oblique

Secondary muscles

rectus abdominis
transverse abdominis

Equipment

Pull-Up Bar (Doorframe)

Starting position

  • Set up for Hanging Windshield Wiper Bent Knee with Pause in proper position.

Setup

  • Prepare body and equipment for Hanging Windshield Wiper Bent Knee with Pause.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Hang from bar with knees bent at 90 degrees and legs raised to horizontal.
  2. Step 2: Rotate bent legs to one side until thighs are nearly parallel to floor.
  3. Step 3: Pause for 2 seconds at the endpoint.
  4. Step 4: Return through center and rotate to opposite side, pausing again.

Coaching cues

  • The pause is what makes it effective
  • Control the speed throughout

Breathing

  • Breathe steadily; never hold breath during static holds.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion with control at all times.

Tempo

  • Controlled throughout; hold positions deliberately.

Common mistakes

  • Swinging to generate momentum
  • Not pausing at the endpoint

Safety

  • Build prerequisite strength before attempting advanced variations.
  • Stop if joint pain occurs.
