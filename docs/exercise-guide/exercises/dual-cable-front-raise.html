Dual Cable Front Raise

strengthbeginnerisolationpushbilateral

The dual cable front raise uses a cable behind the body from two low pulleys — one handle in each hand — raising both arms simultaneously to shoulder or eye level. The cables provide constant tension throughout the full range of motion, unlike dumbbells which unload near the top, making this variation superior for hypertrophy through the anterior deltoid and upper chest.

Primary muscles

deltoid anterior

Secondary muscles

deltoid lateral
trapezius upper

Stabilizers

erector spinae

Equipment

Cable Machine (High Pulley)

Starting position

  • Stand between two low cable pulleys with one handle in each hand. Face away from the cable stack. Arms at sides, cables running from behind.

Setup

  • Set both pulleys to the lowest position. Adjust cable weights equally. Stand close to the cable stack initially to gauge tension.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with arms at sides, cables taut running from behind.
  2. Step 2: Raise both arms simultaneously forward and upward to shoulder height.
  3. Step 3: Maintain slight elbow bend throughout — not fully locked, not deeply bent.
  4. Step 4: Hold 1 second at shoulder height.
  5. Step 5: Lower under control.

Coaching cues

  • Thumbs slightly up at the peak
  • No torso lean — strict upright posture
  • Lead with the pinkies as the arms rise

Breathing

  • Exhale on the raise. Inhale lowering.

Range of motion

  • Arms at sides to shoulder height.

Tempo

  • 2-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Raising above shoulder height — this recruits traps and reduces anterior delt emphasis
  • Using momentum from the torso — strict movement only

Safety

  • Light loads recommended — anterior deltoid isolation exercises have high leverage disadvantage
  • Avoid if experiencing shoulder impingement

Training

  • hypertrophy: 12–20 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
