Zercher Yoke Walk with Chains

strengthexpertcompoundcarrybilateral

A Zercher yoke walk with chains added to the implement for accommodating resistance. The chains increase weight as the athlete stands taller and decrease weight in the bottom position. This variation trains the entire walk with varied loading and builds lockout strength.

Primary muscles

rectus abdominis
transverse abdominis

Secondary muscles

erector spinae
rectus femoris

Stabilizers

internal oblique

Equipment

Standard Barbell

Starting position

  • Set up for Zercher Yoke Walk with Chains in proper strongman position.

Setup

  • Prepare all equipment and body position for Zercher Yoke Walk with Chains.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Attach chains to Zercher yoke. Cradle yoke in elbow creases.
  2. Step 2: Stand and step clear of rack as chains lift from floor.
  3. Step 3: Walk briskly as chains swing and provide dynamic resistance.
  4. Step 4: Set down at end of walk managing chain pendulum motion.

Coaching cues

  • Smooth steps reduce chain oscillation
  • Keep elbows tight to cradle bar

Breathing

  • Brace core maximally before every lift. Breathe between reps if needed.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion specific to the implement being used.

Tempo

  • Explosive concentric phase, controlled eccentric.

Common mistakes

  • Walking too fast increasing chain swing
  • Losing Zercher position

Safety

  • Use collars on all loaded barbells.
  • Ensure clear path for carries.
  • Have spotters for overhead movements.

Training

  • strength: 1–5 reps
  • power: 1–3 reps
  • strength: 3–6 sets
  • power: 3–5 sets
