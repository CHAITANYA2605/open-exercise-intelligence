Sandbag Bear Hug Carry

strengthintermediatecompoundcarrybilateral

A strongman carry where a sandbag is cradled in a bear hug position against the chest and carried for distance or time. The soft and shifting sandbag makes this considerably harder than carrying rigid implements. This builds grip endurance, core bracing, and mental toughness.

Primary muscles

transverse abdominis
erector spinae

Secondary muscles

rectus abdominis
internal oblique

Equipment

Slam Ball

Starting position

  • Set up for Sandbag Bear Hug Carry in proper strongman position.

Setup

  • Prepare all equipment and body position for Sandbag Bear Hug Carry.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Deadlift sandbag up to lap position.
  2. Step 2: Bear hug bag tightly against chest with arms wrapped underneath.
  3. Step 3: Stand upright and walk for target distance or time.
  4. Step 4: Set bag down with control.

Coaching cues

  • Squeeze bag as hard as possible
  • Walk tall with upright torso

Breathing

  • Brace core maximally before every lift. Breathe between reps if needed.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion specific to the implement being used.

Tempo

  • Explosive concentric phase, controlled eccentric.

Common mistakes

  • Letting bag slip too low
  • Bent-over carry posture

Safety

  • Use collars on all loaded barbells.
  • Ensure clear path for carries.
  • Have spotters for overhead movements.

Training

  • strength: 1–5 reps
  • power: 1–3 reps
  • strength: 3–6 sets
  • power: 3–5 sets
