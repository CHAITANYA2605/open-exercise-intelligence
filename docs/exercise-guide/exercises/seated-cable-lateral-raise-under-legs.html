Seated Cable Lateral Raise (Cable Under Legs)

strengthbeginnerisolationpushunilateral

The seated cable lateral raise with the cable running under the legs is performed seated on a bench beside a cable, with the cable passing beneath the thighs to the working hand. This setup creates a unique loading angle where the cable pulls the working arm downward and inward at the bottom of the range, then shifts to a lateral pull at the top — providing a consistent tension profile that keeps the medial deltoid loaded at the bottom of the movement.

Primary muscles

deltoid lateral

Secondary muscles

trapezius upper

Equipment

Cable Machine (High Pulley)

Starting position

  • Sit on a flat bench beside a low cable pulley. The cable runs underneath the thighs to the working hand. Grip the handle with the far hand, arm resting across the thighs.

Setup

  • Sit close enough to the cable that the cable can run naturally under the legs. This may require a low bench or the floor.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin seated with the cable running under the thighs, working arm at side.
  2. Step 2: Raise the working arm out to the side and upward to shoulder height.
  3. Step 3: Hold 1 second at peak.
  4. Step 4: Lower under control — the cable will pull from beneath as you descend.
  5. Step 5: Complete all reps then switch sides.

Coaching cues

  • Lead with the elbow
  • Feel the tension from the very start of the movement
  • No torso lean or swing

Breathing

  • Exhale raising. Inhale lowering.

Range of motion

  • Arm across body to shoulder height laterally.

Tempo

  • 2-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Sitting too far from the cable — the cable should run smoothly under the legs
  • Raising above shoulder height — stop at horizontal for medial delt emphasis

Safety

  • Ensure the cable path under the legs does not create a tripping hazard
  • Light loads — isolation exercise

Training

  • hypertrophy: 12–20 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
