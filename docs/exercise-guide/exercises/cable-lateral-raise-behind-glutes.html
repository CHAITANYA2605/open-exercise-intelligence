Cable Lateral Raise (Cable Behind Glutes)

strengthbeginnerisolationpushunilateral

The cable lateral raise with the cable passing behind the glutes positions the cable at the low-back or glute level before the lift, changing the angle of resistance throughout the raise. This positioning maintains lateral deltoid tension through the bottom portion of the movement — where dumbbell raises have minimal tension — making it mechanically advantageous for complete medial deltoid loading across the full range.

Primary muscles

deltoid lateral

Secondary muscles

trapezius upper

Stabilizers

erector spinae

Equipment

Cable Machine (High Pulley)

Starting position

  • Stand side-on to a low cable pulley. Reach behind the body and grasp the cable handle with the far hand, so the cable passes behind the glutes. Stand tall.

Setup

  • The cable should run behind the lower back and under the opposite arm's side. Grip the handle with the arm that is furthest from the cable stack.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with the cable behind the glutes, arm at the side.
  2. Step 2: Raise the working arm out to the side and upward to shoulder height.
  3. Step 3: The cable will pull from behind — this unique angle provides early-range tension.
  4. Step 4: Lower under control.
  5. Step 5: Complete all reps then turn and repeat on the other side.

Coaching cues

  • Cable runs behind your back — not to the side
  • Lead with the elbow, not the hand
  • Feel the tension from the very bottom of the movement

Breathing

  • Exhale raising. Inhale lowering.

Range of motion

  • Arm at side to shoulder height.

Tempo

  • 2-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Not actually passing the cable behind the glutes — the cable must run behind the body
  • Excessive lean toward the cable — maintain upright posture

Safety

  • Light loads — this is a finisher or detail exercise, not a strength builder
  • Ensure cable attachment does not catch on clothing

Training

  • hypertrophy: 12–20 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
