Overhead Cable Tricep Extension

strengthbeginnerisolationpushbilateral

A tricep isolation exercise performed with a rope or straight bar attachment at a high cable pulley, extending the arms overhead. The overhead position places the long head of the triceps in a lengthened position, maximizing the stretch and providing greater hypertrophic stimulus compared to pushdown variations.

Primary muscles

triceps long head
triceps lateral head
triceps medial head

Secondary muscles

brachialis

Stabilizers

transverse abdominis

Equipment

Cable Machine (High Pulley)

Starting position

  • Stand facing away from a high cable pulley holding a rope attachment behind your head, elbows bent and pointing forward.

Setup

  • Attach a rope to the high cable pulley. Face away from the machine. Grab the rope and position it behind your head.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Keep the upper arms stationary and close to the head throughout.
  2. Step 2: Extend the arms forward and downward, straightening the elbows.
  3. Step 3: Fully extend the elbows at the bottom of the movement.
  4. Step 4: Bend the elbows to return the hands behind the head under control.

Coaching cues

  • Elbows stay up and forward
  • Full stretch in the long head

Breathing

  • Exhale on the extension. Inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full extension of the elbows at the front. Full elbow flexion at the back — elbows well above the head.

Tempo

  • 2-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Allowing the upper arms to drop or flare during the movement
  • Using momentum by leaning the torso

Safety

  • Keep the upper arms close to the head and stationary throughout

Training

  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
