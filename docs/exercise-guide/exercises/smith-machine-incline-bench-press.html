Smith Machine Incline Bench Press

strengthbeginnercompoundpushbilateral

The Smith Machine Incline Bench Press uses the fixed bar path of a Smith machine to perform an incline pressing movement targeting the clavicular head of the pectoralis major. The guided bar path removes stabilization demands, allowing greater focus on the upper chest and making it accessible for beginners and useful for high-rep isolation work without a spotter.

Primary muscles

pectoralis major clavicular
pectoralis major sternal

Secondary muscles

deltoid anterior
triceps lateral head

Stabilizers

pectoralis minor

Equipment

Smith Machine
Adjustable Bench (Incline / Decline)

Starting position

  • Position an adjustable bench at 30-45 degrees inside the Smith machine. Align the bar to touch the upper chest. Grip slightly wider than shoulder-width.

Setup

  • Set the Smith machine bar hooks at an appropriate height. Position the bench so that when lying on it, the bar descends naturally to the upper chest without awkward angle. Adjust incline to 30-45 degrees.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Unrack by rotating the bar off the hooks.
  2. Step 2: Lower the bar under control to the upper chest.
  3. Step 3: Press up along the fixed bar path to full extension.
  4. Step 4: Rerack by rotating the bar back onto the hooks after the final rep.

Coaching cues

  • Position the bench so the bar drops straight down to your upper chest
  • Drive through the chest — do not lead with shoulders

Breathing

  • Inhale before lowering. Exhale at the top.

Range of motion

  • Full touch to upper chest at bottom; full elbow extension at top.

Tempo

  • 3-1-1-0

Common mistakes

  • Setting the bench at too steep an angle, shifting load from chest to shoulders
  • Placing the bench too far forward or backward in the Smith machine, creating an unnatural bar path angle

Safety

  • Ensure the bench is positioned so the natural Smith machine bar path aligns with the upper chest, not the throat
  • Learn to rerack confidently before training heavy

Training

  • strength: 5–8 reps
  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • endurance: 15–20 reps
  • strength: 3–5 sets
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
  • endurance: 2–3 sets
