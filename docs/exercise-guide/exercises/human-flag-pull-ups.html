Human Flag Pull-Ups

strengthexpertcalisthenicsanti-rotationbilateral

An extreme variation where pull-ups are performed while maintaining the human flag position on a vertical pole. The body stays horizontal while the athlete performs elbow flexion and extension. This movement is accessible only to elite calisthenics athletes with established flag holds.

Primary muscles

internal oblique
external oblique

Secondary muscles

latissimus dorsi
rectus abdominis

Equipment

No Equipment (Bodyweight)

Starting position

  • Set up for Human Flag Pull-Ups in proper position.

Setup

  • Prepare body and equipment for Human Flag Pull-Ups.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Establish full human flag hold on vertical pole.
  2. Step 2: From flag position, bend both elbows to perform a pull-up laterally.
  3. Step 3: Extend arms back to flag position.
  4. Step 4: Maintain horizontal body throughout the movement.

Coaching cues

  • Fully master the static flag before this
  • Lateral pull direction, not vertical

Breathing

  • Breathe steadily; never hold breath during static holds.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion with control at all times.

Tempo

  • Controlled throughout; hold positions deliberately.

Common mistakes

  • Dropping hips during the pull
  • Attempting without stable flag hold

Safety

  • Build prerequisite strength before attempting advanced variations.
  • Stop if joint pain occurs.

Training

  • strength: 1–5 reps
  • hypertrophy: 5–10 reps
  • strength: 3–5 sets
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
