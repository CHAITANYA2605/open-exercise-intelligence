Single-Arm Overhead Cable Tricep Extension

strengthbeginnerisolationpushunilateral

The single-arm overhead cable extension uses one hand on a rope or handle attached to a low cable, extending one arm overhead at a time. Training each arm independently allows the lifter to identify and correct strength imbalances between the triceps. The overhead position maximally stretches the long head, and the unilateral loading may allow a greater range of motion and more focused mind-muscle connection.

Primary muscles

triceps long head

Secondary muscles

triceps lateral head
triceps medial head

Equipment

Cable Machine (Low Pulley)

Starting position

  • Attach a single handle to a low cable. Face away from the machine. Grab the handle with one hand and bring it behind the head with the elbow bent overhead.

Setup

  • The free hand can grip the working upper arm for stability or hold the cable for balance.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with the working elbow bent, hand behind the head.
  2. Step 2: Extend the elbow overhead until the arm is fully straight.
  3. Step 3: Hold at full extension for one second.
  4. Step 4: Return slowly to the starting bent position.

Coaching cues

  • Elbow stays pointing up
  • Feel the stretch at the bottom
  • Full lockout overhead

Breathing

  • Exhale on extension, inhale on return.

Range of motion

  • Full elbow flexion with stretch at the bottom to full overhead extension.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Elbow flaring to the side
  • Torso rotating to assist
  • Insufficient range of motion at the bottom

Safety

  • Ensure the elbow points straight up at the starting position

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
