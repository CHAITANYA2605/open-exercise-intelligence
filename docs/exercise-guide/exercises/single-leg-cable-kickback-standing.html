Standing Single-Leg Cable Kickback

strengthbeginnerisolationhingeunilateral

A glute isolation exercise performed standing, using a cable ankle attachment to provide resistance as the leg is extended behind the body. The cable offers continuous resistance throughout the kickback motion, making it superior to bodyweight donkey kicks for progressive overload and glute isolation.

Primary muscles

gluteus maximus

Secondary muscles

biceps femoris long head
gluteus medius

Stabilizers

transverse abdominis
erector spinae

Equipment

Cable Machine (Low Pulley)

Starting position

  • Attach an ankle strap to the low pulley and around one ankle. Face the machine and hold the frame for support.

Setup

  • Attach the ankle cuff to the low cable pulley and around one ankle. Stand facing the machine, feet hip-width apart.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Maintain a slight forward lean from the hips for stability.
  2. Step 2: Keeping the working leg straight, extend it backward behind your body.
  3. Step 3: Squeeze the glute maximally at full hip extension.
  4. Step 4: Return the leg forward in a controlled manner and repeat.

Coaching cues

  • Squeeze the glute at the back
  • Keep the torso stable — no twisting

Breathing

  • Exhale as the leg extends back. Inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full hip extension at the back — squeeze the glute at peak contraction.

Tempo

  • 2-0-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Swinging the leg rather than using controlled muscle contraction
  • Rotating the hip or twisting the torso

Safety

  • Keep the movement controlled and isolated to the hip joint — do not use momentum

Training

  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • endurance: 15–25 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
  • endurance: 2–3 sets
