Dumbbell Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullalternating

The dumbbell curl trains the biceps brachii and brachialis using independent dumbbells, allowing the wrist to supinate freely through the range of motion which maximizes biceps activation. It can be performed with both arms simultaneously or alternating, and the independent loading helps identify and correct biceps strength imbalances.

Primary muscles

biceps brachii long head
biceps brachii short head
brachialis

Secondary muscles

brachioradialis

Equipment

Dumbbell (Fixed)

Starting position

  • Stand or sit holding a dumbbell in each hand at the sides with a neutral or supinated grip, elbows against the sides.

Setup

  • Can be performed seated or standing. Seated reduces the temptation to use body swing.

Steps

  1. Curl one dumbbell (or both) upward, rotating the wrist to full supination as the dumbbell rises.
  2. Fully contract the bicep at the top.
  3. Lower under control, rotating back to neutral if alternating.
  4. Repeat on the other side if alternating.

Coaching cues

  • Turn the pinky toward the ceiling at the top to maximize supination
  • Keep the elbow pinned — it should not drive forward

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on the descent.

Range of motion

  • Full extension at the bottom with full supination at the top.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Using body swing to lift the dumbbell
  • Not fully supinating the forearm during the curl

Safety

  • If using alternating technique, maintain a neutral wrist at the bottom

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
