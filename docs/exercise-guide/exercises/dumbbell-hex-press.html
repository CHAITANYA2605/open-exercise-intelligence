Dumbbell Hex Press

strengthintermediateisolationpushbilateral

A chest press variation where two dumbbells are pressed together throughout the entire movement, maintaining constant medial pressure. This squeeze activates the pectoralis major's adduction function and significantly increases sternal head activation, making it a highly effective inner chest exercise.

Primary muscles

pectoralis major

Secondary muscles

triceps long head
triceps medial head
anterior deltoid

Stabilizers

serratus anterior
transverse abdominis

Equipment

Dumbbell (Fixed)
Flat Bench

Optional

Adjustable Bench (Incline / Decline)

Starting position

  • Lie on a flat bench holding two dumbbells pressed together at chest height with the flat ends facing each other (hence hex).

Setup

  • Select two hex-sided dumbbells that can be pressed together stably. Lie on a flat bench and press them together.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Keep the dumbbells pressed together throughout — do not let them separate.
  2. Step 2: Lower the dumbbells as a single unit to your chest, elbows tucked at about 45 degrees.
  3. Step 3: Press back up to lockout while continuing to squeeze the dumbbells together.
  4. Step 4: Maintain medial pressure throughout the entire set.

Coaching cues

  • Squeeze the dumbbells together the whole time
  • Inner chest is working

Breathing

  • Inhale on the descent. Exhale on the press.

Range of motion

  • Lower until the dumbbells touch the chest. Full extension at the top.

Tempo

  • 2-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Letting the dumbbells separate, reducing the adduction stimulus
  • Flaring the elbows wide

Safety

  • Use hex dumbbells or dumbbells with flat sides — round dumbbells will roll and may be unsafe

Training

  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
  • Rest: {'hypertrophy': 90}
