Decline EZ-Bar Skull Crusher

strengthintermediateisolationpushbilateral

The decline EZ-bar skull crusher uses a decline bench (typically -15 to -30 degrees) which moves the target area from the forehead downward, allowing the bar to travel further behind the head at the bottom of the movement. The decline angle places the medial and lateral heads under greater relative load compared to the flat version, and many lifters find the decline position more comfortable on the elbows. The extended range behind the head increases tricep stretch.

Primary muscles

triceps lateral head
triceps medial head

Secondary muscles

triceps long head

Equipment

EZ-Curl Bar
Adjustable Bench (Incline / Decline)

Starting position

  • Set a bench to a slight decline. Secure feet. Lie back and have a spotter hand the EZ-bar to full extension above the lower chest/face.

Setup

  • Foot hooks are needed for the decline position. Keep the decline moderate (around 15 degrees).

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with EZ-bar above the chest/lower face at full elbow extension.
  2. Step 2: Lower the bar by bending only the elbows, letting the bar travel toward and behind the head.
  3. Step 3: Lower as far as comfortable without straining the elbows.
  4. Step 4: Press back to the starting position by extending the elbows.

Coaching cues

  • Upper arms fixed — perpendicular to bench
  • Let the bar go behind the head
  • Drive up through the triceps

Breathing

  • Inhale on descent, exhale on press.

Range of motion

  • Full extension to bar behind the top of the head.

Tempo

  • 2-0-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Letting the bar travel too far behind the head with heavy loads
  • Upper arms moving — keep them fixed

Safety

  • The decline position makes the bar harder to control if dropped — always use a spotter

Training

  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
