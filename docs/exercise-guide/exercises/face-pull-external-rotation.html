Face Pull with External Rotation

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

A cable face pull variation where the hands are pulled to face level and then actively rotated outward at the end of the movement. The external rotation component powerfully targets the infraspinatus, teres minor, and rear deltoids, making this one of the most effective exercises for shoulder health, posture correction, and rotator cuff strengthening.

Primary muscles

posterior deltoid
infraspinatus

Stabilizers

transverse abdominis

Equipment

Cable Machine (High Pulley)

Starting position

  • Stand facing a high cable pulley with a rope attachment at face height. Grip both ends of the rope with palms facing inward.

Setup

  • Attach a rope to the high cable pulley set at upper chest or face height. Stand back enough to create tension with arms extended.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Pull the rope toward your face, allowing the elbows to flare out to the sides.
  2. Step 2: As the rope reaches face level, rotate the hands outward so the knuckles point toward the ceiling.
  3. Step 3: Hold the peak contraction briefly, squeezing the rear delts and rotator cuff.
  4. Step 4: Return to the starting position under control.

Coaching cues

  • Pull to the face, then rotate the hands out
  • Light weight, full range

Breathing

  • Exhale during the pull and rotation. Inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full extension at the start. Rope pulled to face level with hands rotated outward at the end.

Tempo

  • 2-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Pulling too heavy and using momentum
  • Skipping the external rotation component at the end

Safety

  • Use a light weight to ensure full range and proper activation
  • This exercise is excellent for shoulder health — program it regularly

Training

  • strength: 3–8 reps
  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • endurance: 15–25 reps
  • strength: 3–5 sets
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
  • endurance: 2–3 sets
