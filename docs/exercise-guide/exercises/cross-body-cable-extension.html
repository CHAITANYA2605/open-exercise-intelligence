Cross-Body Cable Tricep Extension

strengthintermediateisolationpushunilateral

The cross-body cable extension uses a cable set at shoulder height on one side while the lifter stands facing away or perpendicular, extending the arm across the body in a diagonal path. The cross-body path creates a unique torque on the tricep and allows the elbow to travel through a wider arc than standard pushdowns. It is an excellent cable tricep variation for targeting the lateral and medial heads with constant tension.

Primary muscles

triceps lateral head
triceps medial head

Secondary muscles

triceps long head

Equipment

Cable Machine (High Pulley)

Starting position

  • Attach a single handle to a high cable on one side. Stand perpendicular to the cable or facing away from the cable on the same side. Grasp the handle with the near hand, elbow bent.

Setup

  • The arm extends diagonally across the body — the cable should pull from the side, not from above.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with the elbow bent and the handle near the shoulder.
  2. Step 2: Extend the elbow, pressing the handle diagonally across and downward.
  3. Step 3: Hold at full extension for one second.
  4. Step 4: Return with control to the bent position.

Coaching cues

  • Extension only — don't rotate
  • Squeeze at lockout
  • Upper arm stays still

Breathing

  • Exhale on extension, inhale on return.

Range of motion

  • Full elbow flexion to full extension.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Using the shoulder to push rather than tricep extension
  • Torso rotating

Safety

  • Keep the shoulder stable — only the elbow joint moves

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
