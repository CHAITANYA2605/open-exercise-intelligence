Cable EZ-Bar Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

The cable EZ-bar curl attaches an EZ-curl bar to a low cable pulley and performs a bilateral curl. The cable maintains constant tension throughout the full arc — unlike free weight EZ-bar curls which go slack at the bottom — while the EZ-bar's angled grip reduces wrist stress. This combination provides constant tension with wrist-friendly positioning, making it a highly effective and comfortable hypertrophy exercise.

Primary muscles

biceps brachii long head
biceps brachii short head

Secondary muscles

brachialis
brachioradialis

Equipment

Cable Machine (Low Pulley)
EZ-Curl Bar

Starting position

  • Attach an EZ-bar to the low cable pulley. Stand facing the cable machine. Grasp the EZ-bar with a semi-supinated grip. Arms extended toward the pulley.

Setup

  • Clip the EZ-bar to the low pulley carabiner. Stand one step back from the stack.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with arms extended, EZ-bar at thigh level, cable taut.
  2. Step 2: Curl the bar upward by flexing the elbows, maintaining semi-supinated grip.
  3. Step 3: Curl to maximum elbow flexion.
  4. Step 4: Return slowly to the starting position.

Coaching cues

  • Feel the tension at the bottom
  • Elbows stay at the sides
  • Squeeze at the peak

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full extension to full flexion with constant cable tension throughout.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Swinging the torso
  • Elbows drifting forward
  • Moving too fast

Safety

  • Ensure the EZ-bar attachment is secure before beginning

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
