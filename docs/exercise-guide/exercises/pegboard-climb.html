Pegboard Climb

strengthexpertcompoundpullbilateral

A climbing movement using wooden pegs inserted into a vertical board where the athlete progresses hand over hand upward. This builds extraordinary grip strength, upper body pulling power, and shoulder stability. A staple of gymnastics strength testing and specialty functional fitness.

Primary muscles

latissimus dorsi
biceps brachii long head

Secondary muscles

rectus abdominis
internal oblique

Equipment

No Equipment (Bodyweight)

Starting position

  • Set up for Pegboard Climb in proper strongman position.

Setup

  • Prepare all equipment and body position for Pegboard Climb.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Grip two pegs at the bottom of the pegboard at shoulder height.
  2. Step 2: Pull body up and insert upper peg into the next hole above.
  3. Step 3: Continue alternating peg placements up the board.
  4. Step 4: Descend with the same alternating pattern under control.

Coaching cues

  • Smooth deliberate movements
  • Descent is as important as ascent

Breathing
