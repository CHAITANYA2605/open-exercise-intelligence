Exercise guide / Waiter Curl

Waiter Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

The waiter curl is performed by holding a weight plate by its rim with both hands underneath, palms facing upward as if carrying a tray. The underhand plate position keeps constant supination tension on the biceps throughout the curl, emphasizing the short head and brachialis. It is a simple, low-setup bicep exercise that also challenges the forearms and wrist stability.

Primary muscles

biceps brachii short head
brachialis

Secondary muscles

biceps brachii long head
brachioradialis

Equipment

Weight Plates

Starting position

  • Stand upright holding a weight plate by the rim with both hands underneath, palms facing up. Elbows at the sides.

Setup

  • Choose a plate with a comfortable grip around the rim. A 10-25kg plate works well for most lifters.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with arms extended, plate hanging below, palms supinated.
  2. Step 2: Curl the plate upward by flexing the elbows, keeping the palms facing up throughout.
  3. Step 3: Curl until maximum elbow flexion, keeping the plate horizontal.
  4. Step 4: Lower slowly back to the starting position.

Coaching cues

  • Palms face the ceiling the whole way
  • Elbows stay at your sides
  • Plate stays level

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full extension to full flexion.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Letting the plate tilt
  • Swinging the torso to assist the curl
  • Elbows drifting forward

Safety

  • Ensure a firm grip on the plate rim before beginning

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–20 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
