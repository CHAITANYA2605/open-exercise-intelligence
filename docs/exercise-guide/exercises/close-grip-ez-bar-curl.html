Close-Grip EZ-Bar Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

The close-grip EZ-bar curl uses the inner grip position on an EZ-curl bar, which externally rotates the shoulders and shifts greater emphasis to the long head of the biceps. The long head contributes to the peak height of the bicep. The EZ-bar's angled grip reduces wrist strain compared to a straight close-grip barbell curl, making it more comfortable for high-volume training.

Primary muscles

biceps brachii long head

Secondary muscles

biceps brachii short head
brachialis

Equipment

EZ-Curl Bar

Starting position

  • Stand holding an EZ-bar with hands on the inner (narrow) grip position, arms extended.

Setup

  • Use the inner grip knurling of the EZ-bar. Stand with feet hip-width, brace the core.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Curl the EZ-bar upward by flexing the elbows.
  2. Step 2: Keep the upper arms stationary at the sides.
  3. Step 3: Curl to full elbow flexion.
  4. Step 4: Lower slowly to the starting position.

Coaching cues

  • Long head focus — feel the stretch at the bottom
  • Elbows stay at the sides
  • Squeeze at the top

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full extension to full flexion.

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Swinging the torso
  • Elbows drifting forward
  • Locking out too aggressively

Safety

  • EZ-bar reduces wrist stress — preferred over close-grip straight bar for comfort

Training

  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
