Exercise guide / Reverse Curl

Reverse Curl

strengthbeginnerisolationpullbilateral

The reverse curl is a curl variation using an overhand (pronated) grip rather than the standard underhand (supinated) grip. This grip change mechanically disadvantages the biceps brachii while placing the brachioradialis and brachialis as the primary movers. It is an important supplementary exercise for building forearm and outer arm thickness and improving wrist and elbow joint resilience.

Primary muscles

brachioradialis
brachialis

Secondary muscles

biceps brachii long head
extensor carpi radialis

Equipment

Standard Barbell

Optional

Dumbbell (Fixed)
EZ-Curl Bar

Starting position

  • Stand upright holding a barbell (or dumbbells) with an overhand (pronated) grip, hands shoulder-width apart. Arms are fully extended, bar at thigh level.

Setup

  • Use a straight barbell, EZ-curl bar, or dumbbells. The load will be significantly lower than a standard curl due to the grip change.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Hold the bar with a pronated (overhand) grip, hands shoulder-width apart. Stand tall, upper arms at the sides.
  2. Step 2: Brace the core and keep the upper arms stationary.
  3. Step 3: Exhale and curl the bar upward to shoulder level, maintaining the overhand grip throughout.
  4. Step 4: Hold briefly at the top, then inhale and lower the bar back to the starting position.

Coaching cues

  • Keep wrists flat — do not let them flex toward you during the curl
  • Lead with the knuckles

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl; inhale on the return.

Range of motion

  • Full arm extension at the bottom; curl to shoulder level at the top.

Tempo

  • 2-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Letting the wrists collapse into flexion during the curl — keep wrists neutral or slightly extended
  • Using the same weight as a supinated curl — reduce load significantly

Safety

  • If wrist discomfort occurs, switch to an EZ-curl bar which allows a semi-pronated grip

Training

  • hypertrophy: 10–15 reps
  • endurance: 15–20 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
  • endurance: 2–3 sets
