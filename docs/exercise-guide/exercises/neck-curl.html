Neck Curl

strengthintermediateisolationpullbilateral

The neck curl is performed lying supine on a bench with the head extending off the edge, using a plate or neck harness to resist cervical flexion. The exercise targets the deep cervical flexors and sternocleidomastoid, building anterior neck strength and stability that supports the cervical spine under load. It is used by combat athletes, football players, and lifters seeking to strengthen the neck musculature for performance and injury prevention.

Flat Bench

Starting position

  • Lie on a flat bench with the head extending off one end. Hold a weight plate against the forehead with both hands, or use a neck harness. Begin with the head in a neutral or slightly extended position.

Setup

  • Begin with no additional weight (bodyweight) or a very light plate (2.5-5 lbs). The neck musculature is not accustomed to direct loading and must be progressed extremely slowly.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Lie supine with the head off the bench edge, holding a plate to the forehead or wearing a neck harness.
  2. Step 2: Begin from the neutral or slightly extended position.
  3. Step 3: Curl the head forward, bringing the chin toward the chest (cervical flexion).
  4. Step 4: Hold briefly at the top.
  5. Step 5: Lower slowly and under full control back to the start.

Coaching cues

  • Slow and controlled — always
  • Build weeks of work before adding weight
  • If it hurts — stop

Breathing

  • Exhale on the curl, inhale on the descent.

Range of motion

  • From neutral or slight extension to full cervical flexion (chin to chest).

Tempo

  • 2-1-3-0

Common mistakes

  • Using too heavy a weight before the neck is conditioned — build progressively over weeks
  • Jerking the head — movement must be slow and controlled
  • Performing this exercise if experiencing any neck pain or disc issues

Safety

  • This exercise is contraindicated with any cervical disc pathology or acute neck pain
  • Progress load extremely slowly — the cervical spine is delicate
  • Always use a spotter or be conservative with plate placement
