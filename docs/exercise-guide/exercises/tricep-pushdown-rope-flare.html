Tricep Rope Pushdown with Flare

strengthbeginnerisolationpushbilateral

A rope pushdown where the hands flare outward at the bottom of the movement to maximize tricep contraction. The flare at lockout adds extra peak tension compared to a standard rope pushdown. This technique is widely used for tricep detail work.

Primary muscles

triceps lateral head
triceps medial head

Secondary muscles

triceps long head

Equipment

Cable Machine (High Pulley)

Starting position

  • Set up for Tricep Rope Pushdown with Flare in the appropriate position.

Setup

  • Prepare equipment and body position for Tricep Rope Pushdown with Flare.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Attach rope to high pulley. Stand with elbows at sides.
  2. Step 2: Push rope down toward thighs extending elbows.
  3. Step 3: At the bottom, actively spread rope ends outward in a flare.
  4. Step 4: Hold the flared position briefly then return with control.

Coaching cues

  • Actively spread the rope
  • Lock elbows to sides throughout

Breathing

  • Exhale during the exertion phase, inhale during the return.

Range of motion

  • Move through full range of motion without compromising joint integrity.

Tempo

  • 2-0-2 for hypertrophy, controlled throughout.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the flare at bottom
  • Swinging the torso

Safety

  • Keep joints aligned throughout the movement.
  • Use controlled weight appropriate for skill level.

Training

  • strength: 3–8 reps
  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • strength: 3–5 sets
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
