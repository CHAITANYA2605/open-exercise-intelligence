Single-Arm Cable Front Raise (Standing)

strengthbeginnerisolationpushunilateral

The single-arm cable front raise performed standing uses a low cable to raise one arm forward to shoulder height, providing constant tension through the anterior deltoid from the bottom of the range (where dumbbell raises are unloaded) through the top. The standing unilateral version also trains core anti-rotation as the cable pulls asymmetrically, adding a secondary stability demand.

Primary muscles

deltoid anterior

Secondary muscles

deltoid lateral

Stabilizers

erector spinae
transverse abdominis

Equipment

Cable Machine (High Pulley)

Starting position

  • Stand facing away from a low cable pulley. Hold the handle with one hand behind the hip. Stand upright.

Setup

  • Set the cable to the lowest position. Use a D-handle attachment. Keep the non-working hand at the side or on the hip.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin with arm behind the hip, cable running beside the body.
  2. Step 2: Raise the working arm forward and upward to shoulder height.
  3. Step 3: Hold 1 second at peak.
  4. Step 4: Lower under control back to the starting position.
  5. Step 5: Complete all reps before switching arms.

Coaching cues

  • Lead with the thumb slightly up
  • No torso swing or lean
  • Feel the front of the shoulder contract at the peak

Breathing

  • Exhale raising. Inhale lowering.

Range of motion

  • Arm behind hip to shoulder height.

Tempo

  • 2-1-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Torso rotating toward the working arm — maintain square hips and shoulders
  • Raising above shoulder height — stop at horizontal for anterior delt emphasis

Safety

  • Light loads — anterior deltoid isolation is a finishing exercise
  • Avoid torso lean or rotation to assist the raise

Training

  • hypertrophy: 12–20 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
