Cobra Push-Up

strengthintermediatecompoundpushbilateral

The cobra push-up begins in a downward-dog position and dips the body forward in a sweeping arc until the chest is near the floor, then extends the spine into a cobra-like backbend. This movement trains the chest at the bottom of the arc while also opening the thoracic spine, shoulders, and hip flexors through a full-body coordinated motion.

Primary muscles

pectoralis major sternal
pectoralis major clavicular

Secondary muscles

deltoid anterior
triceps lateral head
erector spinae

Stabilizers

transverse abdominis

Equipment

No Equipment (Bodyweight)

Starting position

  • Begin in a downward-facing dog position: hips high, arms and legs straight, forming an inverted V with the body.

Setup

  • No equipment needed. Clear enough floor space for a full-body extended position.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Start in a downward-dog position with hips elevated.
  2. Step 2: Bend the elbows and dip the head and chest forward and down toward the floor in a sweeping arc.
  3. Step 3: As the chest passes between the hands, begin extending the elbows and driving the hips toward the floor.
  4. Step 4: Fully extend the arms and open the chest upward into a cobra or upward-dog back extension.
  5. Step 5: Reverse the motion or return to the downward-dog position to complete one rep.

Coaching cues

  • Dive down through the arms, not straight into the floor
  • Open the chest to the sky at the top
  • Think about drawing a 'U' shape with your body

Breathing

  • Exhale as you dip forward and down. Inhale as you extend up into the cobra position.

Range of motion

  • The dip phase brings the chest close to the floor between the hands. The cobra phase extends fully overhead without letting the hips touch down.

Tempo

  • 2-0-2-0

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the arc and just doing an up-down movement
  • Letting the hips touch the floor during the dip phase
  • Not opening the chest fully at the top

Safety

  • Avoid if you have lumbar spine issues — the cobra extension loads the low back
  • Warm up wrists and shoulders before performing
  • Progress from a modified version if thoracic mobility is limited

Training

  • hypertrophy: 8–15 reps
  • endurance: 15–25 reps
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
  • Rest: {'hypertrophy': 60, 'endurance': 30}
