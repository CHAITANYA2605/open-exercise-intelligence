Ab Wheel Rollout with Pause

strengthintermediatecalisthenicsanti-rotationbilateral

An ab wheel rollout with a deliberate pause at the extended position before returning. The pause eliminates the elastic energy stored in the return and forces the core muscles to restart the return from a maximally stretched position. This significantly increases the training effect.

Primary muscles

rectus abdominis
transverse abdominis

Secondary muscles

erector spinae
internal oblique

Equipment

Ab Wheel (Ab Roller)

Starting position

  • Set up for Ab Wheel Rollout with Pause in proper position.

Setup

  • Prepare body and equipment for Ab Wheel Rollout with Pause.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Begin kneeling with ab wheel. Roll out to nearly full extension.
  2. Step 2: Hold the extended position for 3-5 seconds.
  3. Step 3: Without bouncing, engage core to pull wheel back to knees.
  4. Step 4: Complete reps with pause on each extension.

Coaching cues

  • The pause reveals your true rollout capacity
  • Spine neutral at the pause

Breathing

  • Breathe steadily; never hold breath during static holds.

Range of motion

  • Full range of motion with control at all times.

Tempo

  • Controlled throughout; hold positions deliberately.

Common mistakes

  • Bouncing from the extended position
  • Hyperextending lower back at pause

Safety

  • Build prerequisite strength before attempting advanced variations.
  • Stop if joint pain occurs.

Training

  • strength: 1–5 reps
  • hypertrophy: 5–10 reps
  • strength: 3–5 sets
  • hypertrophy: 3–4 sets
