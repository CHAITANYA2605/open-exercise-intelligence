# Licensing

Open Exercise Intelligence (OEI) uses a split licensing model. Different components of this project are released under different licenses to maximize freedom for developers while maintaining clear legal boundaries.

---

## License Summary

| Component | License | SPDX Identifier |
|---|---|---|
| Source code (scripts, SDKs, API) | MIT License | `MIT` |
| Original exercise metadata | Creative Commons Zero v1.0 | `CC0-1.0` |
| Original exercise descriptions | Creative Commons Zero v1.0 | `CC0-1.0` |
| Original muscle diagrams | Creative Commons Zero v1.0 | `CC0-1.0` |
| Original exercise illustrations | Creative Commons Zero v1.0 | `CC0-1.0` |
| Third-party assets | Their original license | See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md |

## Original Exercise Data — CC0 1.0 (Public Domain Dedication)

All original exercise metadata, descriptions, muscle mappings, equipment data, and exercise relationships created by OEI contributors are dedicated to the **public domain** under the Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.

**CC0 is not an open-source license — it is a public domain dedication.**

This means:

- You may use the data for any purpose, including commercial purposes.
- You do not need to ask for permission.
- You do not need to pay royalties.
- You do not need to provide attribution (though it is appreciated).
- You may modify, remix, and redistribute the data without restriction.
- You may incorporate the data into proprietary products.

**To the extent possible under law, the OEI contributors have waived all copyright and related or neighboring rights to the original exercise data.**

Full legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

## Original Artwork — CC0 1.0

Original muscle diagrams and exercise illustrations created specifically for this project are also dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

**This applies only to artwork created originally for OEI.** Third-party artwork has its own license — see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Third-Party Assets

Some assets in this repository may originate from third parties. These assets retain their original licenses and are **not** covered by CC0 or MIT.

Every third-party asset must have an entry in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) that documents:

- The asset identifier.
- The original source.
- The license.
- Whether commercial use is permitted.
- Whether redistribution is permitted.
- Required attribution text.

**Assets without documented provenance are not accepted into official releases.**

If a third-party asset's license does not permit commercial redistribution, it will not be included in the main dataset. It may be listed in a separate "restricted" index for reference only.

## What This Means for Commercial Use

You may:

- Integrate the OEI dataset into a commercial fitness application.
- Use OEI exercise descriptions in your app without paying royalties.
- Distribute the dataset as part of a paid product.
- Use the SDK source code in a proprietary application.
- Build and sell a product based entirely on OEI.

You may not:

- Claim copyright over the original OEI dataset.
- Falsely represent third-party assets as your own work.
- Strip attribution requirements from CC-BY-4.0 documentation.
- Redistribute third-party assets in ways that violate their licenses.

## Contributor License Agreement

By submitting original content (exercise data, descriptions, artwork) to OEI, contributors agree to dedicate their contribution to the public domain under CC0 1.0 to the extent possible under applicable law.

If you cannot or do not wish to dedicate your contribution to the public domain, do not submit it. Instead, contact the maintainers to discuss alternative arrangements.

For source code contributions, contributors agree to license their code under the MIT License.
